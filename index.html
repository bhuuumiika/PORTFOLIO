About

I'm drawn to the defensive side of security: spotting something wrong in a stream of events, working out what happened, and automating the boring parts so a human can focus on the interesting ones. Security operations, threat detection and digital forensics are where I want to build my career.

I learn best by doing. Most of what I know comes from hands-on labs, competitions and projects where I had to make something work and then explain why it works.

I'm also looking at AI and machine learning from a security angle: how these systems are built end to end, and what new weaknesses AI-powered technology introduces.

Where I'm heading: a solid foundation in IT and security, built through practical industry experience and a deeper understanding of how systems, applications and networks fit together.

Currently building and learning

  • Security monitoring Turning raw traffic and logs into alerts that mean something
  • Incident investigation Reconstructing what happened from the evidence left behind
  • Security automation Python tooling for repeatable checks and triage
  • AI/ML security How AI workflows work and where they can be attacked
